## Reference data
Photon attenuation and stopping-power data are derived from NIST XCOM (Hubbell & Seltzer),
NIST PSTAR and ESTAR — works of the U.S. Government, not subject to copyright in the United States.
Tissue compositions follow ICRU Report 44; dose limits and tissue weighting factors follow ICRP 103.
Typical depth-dose and relaxation values are cited from the literature named in the app.
